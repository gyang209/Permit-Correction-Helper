# [Permit Correction Helper]: AI for Housing Development Permit Resubmissions

Client: City of San José, Housing Development
Team: [Gabrielle, Frank, Jet, Anthony, Matt], Aug–Oct 2026
My part: I helped design the webpage and conducted the feasibility analysis.

## The problem
Homeowners struggle to get past the initiation phase of housing development projects with submitting permit applications that contain missing or incorrect information, resulting in many back and forth with city reviewers. Our project brings better guidance for permit applicants allowing for more clarity for applicants and a reduced amount of back and forth.

## What we built
- The app scans the rejected application that users upload and searches available sources of guidance given by the city.
- Users will recieve a list of what areas need to be reworked and how they could be reworked.
- A human user still decides whether the information is accurate to specified requirements.

## What I would do next
Our prototype needs better scope guidance as the information that our project receives from its searches are from public sources that make contain inaccurate or outdated inforamtion.
